Privacy Policy

Second Brain is a personal application used only by its developer, David Noyes. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

With David's permission, the app has read-only access to his own Gmail messages and Google Calendar events. It cannot send, modify, or delete email or calendar data.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is read only to summarize it and flag items for David's own review.
  • To produce summaries, the text of relevant messages and events is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service for processing on David's behalf. It is not sent to any other third party.
  • Data is otherwise stored only on David's own computer. It is not sold or disclosed to anyone else.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, and is not used by this app to train generalized AI models.

Revoking access

David can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
